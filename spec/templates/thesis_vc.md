# VC Fund Scoring Rubric

Go threshold: 17/25

## Dimensions (0-5 each)

### 1. Portfolio fit
<!-- Does the fund's existing portfolio suggest appetite for your category? -->

### 2. Active deployment
<!-- Is the fund actively investing from a current vehicle? -->

### 3. Stage and check fit
<!-- Does the fund's stated stage and check size match your round? -->

### 4. Mission alignment
<!-- Does the fund have a thesis that includes your sector? -->

### 5. Network and warm-intro path
<!-- Is there a realistic path to get in front of the right partner? -->
