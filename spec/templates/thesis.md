# Angel Investor Scoring Rubric

Go threshold: 17/25

## Dimensions (0-5 each)

### 1. Domain fit
<!-- How well does the investor's background / portfolio match your sector? -->

### 2. Stage fit
<!-- Does the investor write checks at your stage and size? -->

### 3. Mission alignment
<!-- Does the investor care about the problem you're solving? -->

### 4. Network value
<!-- Can this investor open doors (customers, co-investors, press)? -->

### 5. Reachability
<!-- Is there a realistic path to a warm or cold intro? -->
